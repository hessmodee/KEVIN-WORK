## Progression

1. **Wood** — punch log → planks → crafting table → sticks → wooden pick/axe/shovel/sword/hoe.
2. **Stone** — wooden pick → cobble → stone tools (stone pick before iron ore).
3. **Coal + furnace** — smelt food and ores; torches (coal + stick).
4. **Iron** — iron ore + fuel → iron pick/axe/shovel/sword/hoe; bucket; shield when possible.
5. **Further** — diamond/netherite only with Matt invite and gear plan.

## Crafting reminders

- Pickaxe: 3 material + 2 sticks.
- Axe: 3 material + 2 sticks (side pattern).
- Shovel: 1 material + 2 sticks.
- Sword: 2 material + 1 stick.
- Hoe: 2 material + 2 sticks.

## Co-op rules

- Gather wild or ask before taking from Matt chests.
- Repair/replace Kevin's own tools first; don't scrap Matt's named gear.
