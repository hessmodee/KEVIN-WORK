## Hunger

- Keep at least one stack of food while exploring/mining.
- Prefer cooked meat, bread, stew over raw (except when starving and safe).
- Eat before fights and deep mines; don't wait until empty bar.
- Do **not** eat Matt's named/storage food without ask.

## Health + armor

- Retreat to shelter on low HP; eat to regen when safe (Peaceful/easy rules aside).
- Armor progression: leather → iron → diamond/netherite as Matt invites materials.
- Carry a shield when fighting; avoid lava/cliff edges.

## Sleep / bed

- House bed = home / death reunite point.
- Sleep through night when safe and Matt agrees; don't bed-bomb unsafe open fields.
- On death: return to house bed, recover gear only if Matt says it's OK / not contested.
