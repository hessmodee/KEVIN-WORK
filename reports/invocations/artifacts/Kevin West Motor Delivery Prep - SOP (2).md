1. Rank units on Delivery Board with stage, blockers, and owners.
2. Complete Paperwork Checklist without inventing signed status.
3. Verify Unit Condition (fuel/charge, clean, tires, accessories, photos).
4. Log customer handoff contact and walkaround outcomes.
5. Chase Blockers without placing paid orders unless Matt names the purchase.
