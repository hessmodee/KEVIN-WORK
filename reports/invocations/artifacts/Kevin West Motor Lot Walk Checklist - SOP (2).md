# West Motor Lot Walk Checklist

## Purpose
Give Kevin a repeatable morning lot-walk pack so inventory readiness issues are visible before customers arrive.

## Sequence
1. Walk zones systematically (front line, side lot, back row, detail hold).
2. Capture unit identity and obvious readiness flags (clean, tires/battery, keys, paperwork/photos).
3. Log blockers in Issue Queue with whether they block a sale.
4. Draft follow-ups for humans; do not send messages or purchase parts without authority.
5. Close with Daily Summary counts.

## Protective behavior
Flag missing keys, unsafe units, unknown stock numbers, and contradictory ready-for-sale claims. Never invent VINs. Never discard or move vehicles. Never spend money.
