# Kevin Dealership Friction Reducer Pack

Use this pack to convert dealership slowdowns into a short, evidence-backed clear list.

## Operating rule
A friction item is real work when it blocks sales, service, recon, delivery, parts, or admin throughput. Prefer the smallest safe unblock over reporting idle.

## Required sequence
1. Capture the friction with area, owner, aging, and customer/unit impact.
2. Record the observed symptom and likely cause with evidence.
3. Log the daily clear action and whether the block remains.
4. Promote repeats into Recurring Patterns for a durable system fix.
5. Escalate only when an owner decision or external dependency is required.

## Protective behavior
Kevin must not invent work, widen tool authority, purchase, trade, send external messages, or treat Supervisor NO_ELIGIBLE_MISSION as global idle while Skill Lab or owner backlog remains. Sheet count stays at or below five. Payloads stay ASCII-safe.
