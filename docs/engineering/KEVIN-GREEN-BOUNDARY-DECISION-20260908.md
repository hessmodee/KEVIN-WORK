# GREEN boundary decision — 2026-09-08

Matt asked whether Kevin should broaden GREEN, or step into Yellow, so he can grow faster toward pizza orders, Amazon, voice calls, Minecraft, and surprise useful work.

## Decision

Do **not** move the GREEN/YELLOW line this turn.

Do **use the GREEN already authorized** harder: callable proven skills, Supervisor selection, independent verification, self-repair, and missing-capability acquisition.

## Why this is not the bottleneck

Kevin already has standing owner authorization to do, use, learn, combine, repair, test, prove, and reuse anything legitimately GREEN. GREEN-C already includes proven-skill invocation and known reversible self-improvement. The undying 2026-09-04 card already says not to wait for per-step permission on GREEN or on Yellow *preparation*.

What Kevin cannot yet do is the thing that would make those future surprises real:

1. pick useful work without Bess,
2. invoke an already-PROVEN skill on fresh inputs,
3. verify the artifacts,
4. repair the failure himself,
5. resume the original objective.

Until that flywheel works, granting pizza/Amazon/phone execution would add consequence without adding self-reliance.

## What stays GREEN (lean in)

- Public research, repo/file/log inspection, diagnostics, tests, benchmarks.
- Reversible local artifacts: documents, spreadsheets, prototypes, Skill Lab, backups.
- Proven skill invocation with `create_spreadsheet` / `create_text`.
- Isolated candidate skills and source repairs.
- Preparing Yellow work: carts, drafts, call scripts, Minecraft research, app plans.

## What stays Delegated Yellow (prepare, do not self-execute)

- External sends, public posts, voice calls.
- Account changes, privileged installs.
- Bookings, checkout, purchases, money movement.
- User-data changes outside approved roots.

Yellow execution still needs a scoped grant: action class, target, amount/count, expiry, idempotency, receipt.

## What never becomes self-authorized

Unrestricted shell, credential theft, safety/audit weakening, judge/benchmark mutation, silent permission expansion, fake work, fake proof.

## Comparison to "Henry" style local agents

Useful local agents do recurring real work, keep durable state, verify, and improve procedures. They do **not** start by deleting safety. Kevin is on that path, with a stricter receipt standard: a chat reply is not an outcome. Keep that standard. The missing piece is callable capabilities plus Supervisor using them, not a wider permission surface.

## Next Yellow only after
