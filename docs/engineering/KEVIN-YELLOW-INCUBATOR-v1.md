## Split

| Plane | Kevin may do now | Kevin may not do without a grant |

## Grant shape (required fields)

Every Delegated-Yellow execution grant must name:

- action class
- recipient / merchant / publisher / origin
- amount or count limit
- expiry
- idempotency key
- receipt requirement

Retry of an uncertain external effect is forbidden until an idempotency/receipt decision exists.

## Promotion

A Yellow capability graduates to reusable production GREEN only through an owner-approved category or existing promotion policy, plus exact typed inputs, consequence classification, independent verification, rollback, negative/adversarial tests, and repeated real proof.

Pizza, Amazon checkout, voice calls, and public posting remain Delegated Yellow until that bar is met. Live trades remain reserved.
